## Product in one paragraph

Owners sign up, complete KYB, brand their workspace, create clients, issue invoices with sales
tax, and get paid in USD through five processors: NMI, Stripe Connect, Payoneer, Airwallex and
Rainforest. The platform collects a configurable fee and holds settlement for a configurable
number of days. Clients pay through tokenized links with no login. Operators work from a super
admin console with an approval chain, a credential vault, fraud controls, dispute evidence and a
tamper evident audit chain.

## Out of scope

WhatsApp and SMS automation, payroll, HR, double entry accounting, tax filing and tax reports,
social automation, owner staff seats, and eleven of the previously planned processors. Sales tax
is collected on invoices but never filed.
